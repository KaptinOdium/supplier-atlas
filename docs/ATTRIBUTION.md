# Supplier Atlas sources and attributions

## Screening

Manual agent scans consult the public [AutoStrike calendar](https://kaptinodium.github.io/automobilewatch/latest-calendar.json) for dated labor actions and [Ransomware.live](https://www.ransomware.live/) for reported extortion claims. Each scan records which sources responded. A ransomware listing is a lead for checking a company’s statement and operating status.

Global Incident Tracker supplies public incident reports for discovery. Atlas links each displayed report to its incident page and includes the original publisher when available. Global Incident Tracker credits its data under CC BY 4.0. [Global Incident Tracker](https://globalincidenttracker.com/) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

The Global Disaster Alert and Coordination System (GDACS) supplies public disaster alerts and event footprints. Atlas links reported matches to the GDACS event and identifies the supplier location's precision in the basis for review. [GDACS](https://www.gdacs.org/)

The scheduled industry feed uses the GDELT Global Knowledge Graph to find public news reports. Each report links to its publisher. The browser also checks the GDELT DOC index when the saved public feed is unavailable or stale. DOC results display the time GDELT first saw the article. [GDELT data](https://www.gdeltproject.org/data.html) · [GDELT DOC](https://blog.gdeltproject.org/gdelt-doc-2-0-api-debuts/)

The UK Office for Product Safety and Standards and Transport Canada publish safety and recall notices. The Gazette publishes UK company administration notices. Atlas links each notice to the issuing source. [UK product safety notices](https://www.gov.uk/product-safety-alerts-reports-recalls) · [UK Open Government Licence](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · [Transport Canada recall feeds](https://recalls-rappels.canada.ca/en/rss-feeds) · [The Gazette administration notices](https://www.thegazette.co.uk/all-notices/notice?noticetypes=2410) · [The Gazette data reuse](https://www.thegazette.co.uk/all-notices/content/134)

Saved project and imported registers carry their own evidence references and documented review dates. Owners can inspect the project register's source list after signing in.

## Maps and locations

The detailed map uses OpenFreeMap's Liberty and Dark styles, © OpenMapTiles, with OpenStreetMap data © OpenStreetMap contributors. MapLibre GL JS 6.17.0 renders the map under the BSD 3-Clause license. The browser loads the library from jsDelivr and integrity-checks its stylesheet. Map areas viewed are requested from OpenFreeMap. Supplier markers are drawn locally. [OpenFreeMap](https://openfreemap.org/) · [OpenMapTiles](https://openmaptiles.org/) · [OpenStreetMap copyright](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) · [MapLibre GL JS license](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-gl-js/blob/v6.17.0/LICENSE.txt)

The fallback map uses public-domain Natural Earth country geometry at 1:110 million scale. [Natural Earth terms of use](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/)

The offline gazetteer contains 34,214 GeoNames cities15000 records downloaded on 12 November 2026. Atlas packages city names, alternate names, coordinates, country and region codes, and population in geographic data files. City coordinates indicate approximate locations. GeoNames licenses its data under CC BY 4.0. [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

The optional online lookup uses OpenStreetMap Nominatim. It queries city and country names at the member's request, with up to 20 places per batch, at least 1.2 seconds between requests and an in-memory cache. Members review the results before assigning locations. [Nominatim usage policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/nominatim/) · [OpenStreetMap copyright](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)
